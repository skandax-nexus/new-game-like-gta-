# UI-02 - Art Director visual co-sign, round 1

OWNER: Art Director. Ticket UI-02 (in-game HUD, command card, minimap, battle / hero HUD), reviewed against
ART_BIBLE §3.4, §11, §17 and `docs/art/ui_kit.jpg` / `ui_kit_phone.jpg`.

**Verdict: not approved yet (6/10).** The theme is applied correctly almost everywhere. Lacquer panels, gold
hairlines, lotus corners, torana titles, the parchment event card, carved sandstone age cards and the saffron
primary action all match the kit, and contrast is high. Tabs, slots, meters and kbd chips follow the kit grammar.
The radial wheel is the best piece here. What keeps it from ship quality is a set of visible craft defects on the
most-seen surfaces: chips colliding with icons, truncated labels, minimap controls overlapping the map,
overlays leaking at the edges, and landmark cards with no picture. It also needs the evidence the lead review
asked for.

## Blocking (round 2)

1. **Command-card chips collide (PC, world card).** On Fields, Granary, Market and Workshop, the `Alt+Q`…`Alt+B`
   kbd chips overlap the level badges (`L2`, `L3`). On Fields, Stepwell and Trade they also overlap the tops of
   the icons. The kit grammar is:
   - an 18 x 18 single-letter chip in the top-right corner;
   - the modifier shown once, as an `Alt` chip at the end of the tab row (and in the tooltip);
   - the level badge in the top-left, 4 px inset;
   - the icon box at 28 px, with at least 6 px clearance from both chips.
   Apply the same rule to the army card and the battle Intents page.
2. **Truncated labels on primary surfaces.** Affected:
   - battle cards: `Royal ...` x2 (identical), `Royal G...`, `Horse...`, `Supply ...`, and `ELE` in a strip of
     full words;
   - the battle card slot `Aggressi...`;
   - the landscape world card: `Worksh...`, `Elepha...`, `Engine...`;
   - the portrait forces bar: `Suvarna (y...`.

   Fixes:
   - Formation card title: class short name plus a roman index (`Levy I`, `Levy II`, `Guards`, `Horse`,
     `Elephants`, `Scouts`, `Supply`) in Mukta 600 at 13 px. Put the full name in the tooltip / long-press.
   - Stance slot: `Bold` (the strategic layer's Bold / Steady / Wary).
   - Compact landscape slots: the kit's 700-weight caps codes (SPR ARC GRD CAV CHR ELE ENG SCT SUP, plus WRK MKT
     GRN FLD…), per §11.3.
   - Portrait forces bar: drop `(you)` and mark your own side with the gold `You` badge, or a 2 px gold
     underline under the name.
3. **Phone minimap.**
   - Expanded, it is only about 150 px. The collapse button sits on the top-left rim and the north-up toggle
     covers the right edge of the map.
   - The battle minimap on `quality=low` is an empty brown disc.
   - In landscape, the cards strip overlaps the battle minimap.

   Fixes:
   - Expanded size of at least 220 px in portrait and 180 px in landscape.
   - Both buttons outside the circle (e.g. on the rim at 45 degrees, outside the drawn map).
   - Always draw the flat biome tint plus deploy-zone fills, at every tier.
   - In battle, fit the bounds to the deploy zones plus a 25% margin, so formations draw at least 6 x 3 px with a
     facing tick. Today they are 2-4 px specks in the middle of the PC disc.
   - Tone the PC battle hillshade down to a flat tint with about 15% soft relief. The high-frequency olive noise
     reads as dirt.
4. **Overlay layering leaks.**
   - Battle results (PC): the event-feed toasts are cut off by the modal edge ("ort Wardens flees",
     "he enemy commander…"). Hide or fade the feed under the backdrop, as the world HUD already does.
   - Landscape results: the sticky `Return to Menu` sits directly on the formation rows. Give it a lacquer band
     with a 16 px top fade, and pad the scroller by the footer height.
   - Phone settlement / army drawers: the Chronicle badge (`1`) pokes through the drawer's top-right corner.
     Drawers must stack above the realm-button badges.
5. **Landmark cards have no picture.** Both cards use the same pillar glyph, including The Great Road. The AoE
   landmark choice is a showcase moment. Give each carved card an illustration band of at least 96 x 72 px:
   - Final: a golden-hour thumbnail of the landmark model, an `image` asset from the pipeline (request to
     Technical Art).
   - Interim: a distinct ink silhouette per landmark (a stambha with its capital; a road with a milestone and a
     torana).

   Disabled `Choose site` on sandstone currently reads as a 45%-opacity mud blob. Use a flat-stone disabled skin
   instead: ink at 60% on a flat `--c-sandstone` fill, with no gradient.
6. **Evidence needed to co-sign** (with the lead's three items):
   - the settlement panel's lower half (buildings grid with pips and condition, queues, garrison);
   - an event with advisor portraits and a locked choice with its reason;
   - the prisoners choice.

   In addition, the hero prompt bubble and the lock-on reticle must be captured on their real targets. They are
   currently staged over the hero's head, which is not what the spec asks for. Also add one capture per
   colour-blind mode (protan / deutan / tritan) of the world HUD and the battle HUD. The minimap canvas must
   visibly follow the remaps; in my deutan capture it did not change.

## Blocking (round 3)

**B1. Landmark illustration bands: cultural accuracy and cropping** (`src/ui/components/landmarkArt.ts`,
`.lm-band`). The landmark choice is the showcase moment of the Age panel (AoE IV), and these bands are what players
see first. ART_BIBLE §1.5 and §1.3 apply.

1. **The Great Road reads as Japanese.** The gate is a Shinto torii (two posts, straight double lintel with flared
   kasagi ends), and the road has a white dashed centre line, which is a modern highway marking.

   Redraw:
   - **Gate:** a Sanchi torana. Two square posts with block capitals, and **three curved architraves** that sag
     slightly, each ending in a **spiral volute** beyond the posts. Short vertical struts (3 per bay) between the
     architraves, and a small triratna or wheel finial on top.
   - **Road:** a paved road with no centre markings. Use stone-edge kerbs or a cart-rut texture as two faint parallel
     lines, plus 1-2 milestone pillars and a tree row (ART_BIBLE §8.7: "12 m stone torana ... milestone pillars ...
     paved and tree-lined").
2. **Pillars of the Spoken Law crown each pillar with a "seated lion"**, and at 248 x 88 these read as flames or cones.
   §1.5 bans lion-capital echoes. Our capitals carry bulls, elephants, horses, lotuses or an 8-spoke wheel, and §8.7
   specifies four 14 m pillars (bull, elephant, horse, wheel) around an 18 m central pillar with a gilded wheel.

   Redraw:
   - **Central pillar:** taller, with a bell (inverted lotus) capital, an abacus and an open **8-spoke wheel** disc of
     at least 14 px diameter at band size. Use the `lm-cut` fill for the gaps between the spokes.
   - **Flanking pillars:** a standing **bull** on the abacus (hump and horns, readable in profile), and an
     **elephant**.
3. **The Hall of a Hundred Pillars** has a crenellated (merlon) parapet, so it reads as a fort or a Greek temple.
   §8.7 specifies a timber roof with a central **wagon-vault ridge** and a gilded kalasha row. Replace the merlons with
   a low hipped roof line, a barrel-vault ridge and 5-7 small kalasha finials.
4. **The Salt Caravanserai** uses a tall pishtaq block with an arched opening and crenellations. That is the
   Islamic-era caravanserai grammar, about 1,500 years too late (§1.5; §8.7 says "no arcades"). Redraw it as:
   - a low mud-brick courtyard wall with two square **corner towers**;
   - a **trabeated timber gate** with a flat lintel and projecting beam ends;
   - the camel.
5. **The Hall of the Guilds** has a triangular pediment over a colonnade, which reads as a Greek temple. Redraw it as a
   two-storey timber hall:
   - a **barrel (wagon-vault) roof** whose end is a **gavaksha** (horseshoe chaitya-arch) gable;
   - a projecting first-floor balcony on beam ends;
   - the guild banners.
6. **Cropping, across all 15 bands.** The art is drawn in a 120 x 72 (5:3) viewBox with
   `preserveAspectRatio="xMidYMid slice"`, but the band is about 248 x 88 (2.8:1). About 40% of the height is cut away:
   - the ground strip is lost, so subjects float on the band edge;
   - tops are clipped: the Hundred Pillars finial, the Coin House coin, the Arsenal spears, the Lamp Tower top and the
     canopy.

   Fix:
   - Author the silhouettes in a band-shaped viewBox, e.g. `0 0 240 88`, with the ground at y = 76.
   - Keep the subject inside a safe box of 200 x 64 px centred on x = 120.
   - Or keep 5:3 and use `xMidYMax meet`, letterboxed with the sky gradient extended sideways.
   - Re-capture `age-panel-{pc,portrait,landscape}`, and add one capture per faction's landmark pair (a lab page or
     `__SAMRAJYA__.showScreen` with each faction), so all 15 bands are seen at real size.

   **Acceptance:**
   - every subject has its base on a visible ground strip and its finial at least 4 px below the band top;
   - The Great Road, the Edict Pillars, the Hundred Pillars, the Caravanserai and the Guild Hall match the
     descriptions above;
   - nothing in the set reads as East Asian, Greco-Roman, Persian or Islamic-era.

The final art is still the golden-hour thumbnail of each landmark model (an `image` asset, Technical Art). The
interim silhouettes ship until then, so they must be right.
