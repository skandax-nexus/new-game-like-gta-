## Requested co-signs: rulings

1. **No rising-damp or soot foot bands in tiling wall sets: signed.** These come from the model macro map (uv1) and the `damp_rising` decal.
2. **Saturation above 0.45 (Art Director desk change, made before the TA republish).**
   - I accept these wall materials at their current values, up to 0.60 mean HSV saturation: brick_fired_old (0.59), sandstone_carved_red (0.55) and sandstone_red_ashlar (0.51).
   - These presets come down, because roofs and ground are the largest areas in the RTS frame and sit next to the enemy colour `#e0533d` and saffron:
     - `roof_tile_terracotta` `#a65a3c` -> `#a6644a` (S 0.55)
     - `terrain_laterite_soil` `#8e5038` -> `#8e5a44` (S 0.52)
     - `laterite_block` `#8f4a34` -> `#8f5240` (S 0.55)
   - terrain_grass_lush keeps `#5f6f3a` (0.48), subject to AD-4.
   - I change these in `render/style` (materials.ts and terrain.ts) and regenerate the bible tables. TA-01 re-calibrates the three sets against the new presets in the same republish that B2 requires.
3. **Water masks as cache files: accepted only as an interim step, not signed for ship.** mud_wet and paddy_mud cannot pass as monsoon layers until publish keeps baseColor alpha (CTO request). I will re-check them under varsha once it lands.
4. **Mandana and kolam vocabulary: signed.** Both are sanctioned by §5.5 (rice-paste threshold patterns) and the mud_plaster note. The 8-spoke wheel in `kolam_star` is allowed (§1.5); there is no 24-spoke Ashoka Chakra. Fix the footprints as in AD-3.

## Non-blocking notes

- **basalt_dressed close-up:** smooth slate tiles with cloud stains. Add point-chisel dressing and hand-polished arrises (§8.2).
- **terrain_river_sand:** crest spacing is very regular. Add bifurcating ripples and vary the wavelength (10-20 cm) to avoid moiré at RTS distance.
- **ground_dry_earth close-up:** the white pebbles read as droppings. Make them smaller, fewer and soil-tinted.
- **mud_plaster finger arcs:** they look like stamped combs. Vary the radius, the stroke count and the pressure.
- **roof_tile_terracotta:** hooked lips are barely visible. Strengthen the lip shadow at each overlap.
- **Lime plaster on screen:** sky_day p99 is 224/255 and the maximum is 239, so it never clips. Keep that headroom after B1/B2.
