## Blocking (in addition to the lead's fx_smoke, fx_fire, fx_sparks_embers, fx_dust ring and border-guard items)

1. **Every flipbook quad renders as a visible card at high and ultra.**
   - *Symptom:* each quad shows as a lighter or darker rectangle with a grainy dark halo along its edges. See
     `fx_rain_playback`, `fx_nature_playback`, `fx_dust_playback`, `fx_smoke_sun_east/west`, and
     `ta-07/gtao_cards_high_golden.jpg`.
   - *Cause:* GTAO. The cards disappear at quality=medium, which has no GTAO
     (`gtao_off_medium_golden.jpg`, `rain_medium_no_cards.jpg`). In three r170, GTAOPass hides only Points
     and Lines during its normal/depth pre-pass, so transparent meshes with `depthWrite: false` still occlude.
   - *Fix:* TA-07 tags particle meshes (for example `userData.noAO` or a dedicated layer set by
     `createFlipbookMaterial` / the particle helpers) and documents the tag in ADR 0003. Lead Graphics makes
     `src/render/post.ts` hide tagged or transparent objects in the GTAO pre-pass.
   - *Then:* re-capture every `fx_*_playback` and both smoke sun shots at ultra. No quad edge may be visible:
     an empty quad area must stay within 1/255 of the wall behind it. Until then the smoke lighting test
     cannot be judged.
2. **fx_fire, beyond the lead's points.**
   - (a) *The soot cap glows.* Soot pixels carry straight RGB of about (66, 61, 51), roughly 21% of the
     visible texels, and they receive `emissiveIntensity` 8. The smoke tail therefore glows lilac-grey, which
     is obvious at night (`ta-07/fire_mobile_night.jpg`). Soot must be occlusion only (RGB 0 in the
     premultiplied emission), or move to fx_smoke.
   - (b) *The ramp clips to white.* The bottom ~45% of the flame is flat white. Bible §10 ramp:
     `#fff2c0` core -> `#ffb040` body -> `#e2571e` tongues. At emissive 8, at most ~15% of the flame area
     should clip to white, and readable orange tongues should lick upward.
   - (c) *Frame borders.* Edge alpha reaches 77/255 (1024 atlas) and 104/255 (512 atlas). This shows as a cut
     plus a bleed line at the quad top on mobile. Same rule as the lead's border guard: <= 2/255.
   - *Resolution decision (I own ASSET_LIST):* fx_fire and fx_smoke may ship a **2048 atlas on ultra**
     (256 px frames), because WP7 ultra has ~1.7 MB of headroom. High stays 1024 and mobile 512, since mobile
     is the binding budget (0.58 / 0.60 MB).
3. **A black horizon seam is baked into all 7 new skies.**
   - *Measurement:* row luminance drops at the horizon. noon: 0.453 -> 0.053 -> 0.018, then ramps back up.
     night: 0.119 -> 0.001. dusk: 0.121 -> 0.001. For comparison, the old sky_day goes 0.349 -> 0.295 with
     no dip.
   - *Visible as:* a dark line where ground meets sky in every `sky_*_stupa.png` and `sky_*_cycles.png`.
     ART_BIBLE §4.5 calls out a hard horizon line as a defect.
   - *Fix:* the lower hemisphere continues the horizon radiance and blends to the ground colour over 3-6 deg.
     Within 6 deg below the horizon, no row may be darker than 0.6x the row above it. ambientIrradiance does
     not change, because it integrates only the upper hemisphere.
4. **sky_overcast (P0 monsoon dome) does not read as monsoon.**
   - *Symptom:* a flat blue-grey sheet with a few lighter fair-weather puffs (`sky_overcast_cycles.png`,
     `sky_overcast_stupa.png`, `ta-07/sky_overcast_mobile.jpg`). That is nowhere near clouds 0.95.
   - *Target:*
     - a continuous nimbostratus deck covering >= 90% of the upper hemisphere;
     - darker rain-laden bases at 0.6-0.75x the deck luminance, with ragged lighter thinning toward az 180;
     - a brighter wet-haze band in the last ~8 deg above the horizon, in the fog family `#8c989a` / `#c6cecc`;
     - optionally, 1-2 distant rain shafts.
   - *Constraint:* keep ambientIrradiance at 0.50. This sky carries the monsoon mood and the Giridurg key art.
5. **sky_dawn (P0; opening cinematic and dawn_mist) clouds are flat-fill noise blobs.**
   - *Symptom:* they have one beige colour regardless of sun direction: no lit sun-side rims, no darker
     bases, and no forward-scatter silver lining near the sun at az 95 (`sky_dawn_cycles.png`).
   - *Target:* clouds shade by the sun direction (2-3 step lit/shadow ramp plus a thin backlit rim within
     ~35 deg of the sun) and have eroded, wispy edges. Use the same treatment for dusk, noon and haze when
     convenient.
6. **fx_dust puffs and hoof dust shapes.**
   - *Symptom:* all 32 puff frames have a triangular "sand-pile" silhouette on a hard flat base, and the hoof
     sequence reads as an "S" swirl (`fx_dust_cycles_contact.png`, `fx_dust_playback.png`).
   - *Bible §10:* "low and wide, drifts downwind".
   - *Target:* rounded billowing lobes, width:height >= 1.6:1, rolling outward and downwind. No straight
     base edge longer than 20% of the frame width (soft ground contact). The hoof sequence becomes a low,
     backward-kicked spray that settles into a rolling puff.
7. **fx_sparks_embers colour, together with the lead's border and fringe item.**
   - *Symptom:* slash, parry and finisher cores clip to white, and the finisher reads as a white spoked wheel.
   - *Bible §10:* slash_spark orange-gold, parry_flash gold radial, finisher_burst saffron-gold with a dust
     shockwave.
   - *Target:* white only in the hottest <= 10-15% core. Streaks run from `#e9b949` gold to `#e8892b`
     saffron. The finisher ring and shockwave are saffron-gold, not white spokes.

## Required before WP4/GFX integration (not blocking this sign-off)

- **fx_nature (botanical and cultural accuracy, ART_BIBLE §1.2 pillar 5).**
  - The peepal leaf is missing its defining shape: a broad heart-shaped base on the petiole and a long
    tapering drip-tip. It currently reads as an ellipse with a stalk and a bite.
  - The palash petal should be a curved, beak-like flame-orange petal, not a flat leaf with a midrib.
- **fx_mist.**
  - Sprite 3 ("sheet") is a featureless disc, and sprite 0 shows a circular boundary. Placed in the world,
    these read as round balls.
  - Target: irregular, noise-eroded silhouettes with a 2:1 to 4:1 horizontal aspect (mist lies flat). No
    circular iso-lines visible at 2x. Alpha at the border is 0.
- **fx_rain.** Splash frames 2-7 read as a "[ ]" bucket with vertical walls and a flat base. Target: a
  coronet of 6-10 droplet tips flaring outward, wider at the top.
- **lens_dirt (ultra).** Hundreds of 1-2 px speckles plus hard-rimmed bokeh rings read as a star field under
  bloom. Target: soft smudges, fingerprints and a few irregular bokeh shapes of varied size. Keep rim:centre
  contrast <= 1.3, and drop or soften specks to <= 50 with a falloff of >= 4 px.
- **banner_cloth.** The rest pose is perfectly flat. The low tier uses the static mesh (bible §13), so bake a
  gentle drape: 2-3 vertical folds of 2-4 cm amplitude, plus a slight catenary sag at the free edge.
- **Evidence hygiene.**
  - The boot-splash title is still fading over `off_*.png` and `vat_crowd_mobile.png`. Wait for it to finish.
  - `splat_lookdev.png` under golden is too dark (~10% value) to judge albedo. Use sky_noon with a grey card

## Art Director follow-ups (my own paths, not TA-07)

- **Dusk preset (`src/render/style/lighting.ts`).**
  - *Problem:* sky_dusk is calibrated exactly (0.160), but with sky-only light at exposure 1.8 the frame
    crushes to pure black. Ground measures RGB (5, 1, 3) and the stupa railing (4, 0, 0) under a (90, 78, 123)
    sky. The night frame, lit by the moon key, is brighter than dusk, which breaks the
    golden -> dusk -> night continuity and the "no pure black shadows" rule.
  - *Fix:* raise dusk exposure to about 3.0, or sky irradiance to about 0.24. Target: ground >= 8% on screen.
  - *Also:* re-check dawn's front faces (railing at 4% value when backlit).
- **Signature LUTs.** `docs/art/luts/` does not exist yet, so the interim lut_* files reproduce the full
  GRADE_PRESETS grade on top of `post.setGrade`. I will author residual signature LUTs (warm highlights,
  olive mids, blue-violet shadows) so the 35% LUT pass does not apply the grade twice.
