R2-B2  fx_fire
--------------
(a) Rim block. Source: dome-shaped hot spots (height follows the continuous noise; was a slab with one flat 14 cm top)
    confined to the inner 80 % of the bed ellipse (full to 60 %). Same-scale strips, frames 16-28 at the right of the
    bed: r2_fire_rim_r1.png (round 1: a squat flat-topped block with vertical sides) / r2_fire_rim_r2.png (round 2:
    no block - a low attached shoulder). Full loop: r2_fire_frames_14-48.png, r2_fire_frames_loopseam.png.
    Bake: detached flat top (sub-pixel top edge within 1 px) worst 7.0 % of the frame width (frame 14, a separated
    tongue), gate 8 %; shouldered block top worst 6.6 %. Test: <= 10 % on the shipped ultra atlas. Honest limit: the
    automated measures are lenient - the round-1 block itself scored only 6-8 % (its left side runs into the body
    through a dim valley) - the strips are the evidence.
(b) Chroma. Gradient-mapped: one channel v (sRGB-encoded luminance, +-1 LSB triangular dither) + alpha, lossless on
    every tier; colour = ramp(v), 33 stops fitted to the simulated emission (mean |ramp(v) - simulated| 2.3/255),
    R >= G >= B at every stop.
    Emitting texels whose colour leans green / blue (encoded max >= 8):
      ultra 2048   R1 7866 of 550646  ->  R2 0 of 341304   (stored texels with R != G != B: 0)
      high  1024   R1 3723 of 115267  ->  R2 0 of 52516
      mobile 512   R1 1391 of 34381   ->  R2 0 of 14794
    On screen (added light = shot - backdrop-only shot): dim emission (every channel < 200) leaning green / blue:
      night ultra 0 of 13307, night mobile 0 of 2610, day ultra 0 of 9166 pixels. In the bright core, where the tone
      curve's shoulder drives every channel towards white over a warm backdrop, dG exceeds dR by a few levels on
      1706 / 493 / 1482 pixels - the display transform, not the atlas (it is identical by day and night).
    4x-boosted night crops: r2_fire_night_ultra_x4.png, r2_fire_night_mobile_x4.png (no colour blocks; compare
    docs/art/reviews/ta-07/r2_fire_night_*_chroma_x4.jpg).
    Clipping (fxclip, effect pixels): day 0.0 % near-white / 0.9 % pale core; night 0.0 % / 4.6 % (R1 5.1 / 11.9 %).
    Payload: ultra 260 KB, high 92 KB, mobile 25 KB (R1 lossy rgb 252 / 116 / 48 KB).
