## Data requests (Creative Director, `src/data/**` and DESIGN_SPEC)

Open:

1. **CD-9** (review S1) DESIGN_SPEC §7.8 says Giridurg falls in "52.5 days with one fed engineer formation at siegeStrength 1, 48 for Suvarna at 1.1" - that is 4,200 / 80 with the formation at full strength. The same section charges besiegers **0.4%/day siege attrition** (`RULES.supply.attrition.siege` 0.004), so a fed formation of 40 thins to ~33 men and the walls fall later: in the real tick, Suvarna (1.1) on seeds 1-6: 49, 53, 52, 55, 51, 54 days (x1.1 = 53.9, 58.3, 57.2, 60.5, 56.1, 59.4 at siegeStrength 1). With the attrition switched off the same runs take exactly 48 days (52.8 at siegeStrength 1). Please choose: (a) restate the example as "≈ 50-55 days for Suvarna, 55-60 at siegeStrength 1 (siege attrition included)", or (b) keep 52.5 and exempt a depot-fed siege camp from siege attrition (a rule change: SIM-01 would read e.g. `RULES.supply.attrition.siegeFed` 0). SIM-01 recommends (a): camp disease is the price of a long siege, and two engineer formations (26 days) are the designed remedy.
